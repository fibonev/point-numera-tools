FAQ

What counts as revenue uplift?

Incremental revenue you expect because the hire improves pipeline, conversion, retention, or upsell. Use conservative numbers.

What about non-revenue roles?

Use cost savings or avoided spend as the uplift. If impact is indirect, keep assumptions conservative.

Does this include taxes?

No. This is a baseline and doesn't include tax effects.

When does hiring not make sense?

If monthly net impact is negative or payback is beyond a year, recheck assumptions before hiring.
