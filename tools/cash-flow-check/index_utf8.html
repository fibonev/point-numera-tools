FAQ

What does this cash-flow check do?

It estimates net cash flow, runway, and timing risk from your cash, collections, and cost inputs.

How do timing gaps affect cash?

Longer DSO vs. DPO means you pay bills before collecting cash, increasing buffer needs.

What if my net cash flow is positive?

You’re not burning, but timing gaps can still create short-term crunches.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser and isn’t saved.

Is this financial advice?

No. It’s a quick directional check—talk to your finance advisor for detailed planning.
