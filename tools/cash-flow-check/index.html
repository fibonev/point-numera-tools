FAQ

What does this cash-flow check do?

It estimates net cash flow, runway, and timing gaps from your cash, collections, and cost inputs.

How do timing gaps affect cash?

If DSO is longer than DPO, you pay bills before collecting cash, which increases short-term buffer needs.

What counts as operating costs?

Overheads you pay monthly like payroll, rent, software, and baseline services.

What if my net cash flow is positive?

You're not burning, but timing gaps and volatility can still cause crunches—keep a small buffer.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser and isn't saved.
