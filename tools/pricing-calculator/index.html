FAQ

What does this pricing calculator do?

It estimates margin, break-even units, and monthly profit using your price, variable costs, fixed costs, and sales volume.

What costs count as variable costs?

Costs that increase per unit sold, like materials, packaging, and per-order delivery.

What are fixed costs?

Monthly overheads that don’t change much with sales volume, like rent, software, and base payroll.

What is break-even in units?

The number of units you need to sell so contribution covers fixed costs.

What’s the difference between gross margin and profit?

Gross margin is per-sale contribution before overhead; profit is what’s left after fixed costs.

Is this calculator enough to set my final price?

It’s a baseline. Final pricing often needs discounts, channel fees, VAT, waste, and capacity constraints.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser and inputs aren’t saved.
