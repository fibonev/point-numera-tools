# Point Numera — Lead Magnet Scorecard
> Use this checklist before launching any lead magnet: tool, guide, template, challenge, or calculator.
> Score each criterion 0–5. A total of 41–50 means you are ready to launch.
> Read together with kb-build.md and kb-tools.md.

---

## How to Score

Rate each criterion from **0 to 5**:
- **5** — fully meets the description of a strong example
- **3** — partially there, needs some work
- **0–1** — missing or clearly off

**Maximum score: 50**

---

## The 10 Criteria

### 1. Solves a specific, urgent problem
**What a 5 looks like:** Addresses one pain or desire that your ICP (Ideal Client Profile) is actively searching for right now — not eventually, not theoretically.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** It is unclear what problem it solves, it is generic, or it addresses a "nice to have" rather than a real need.

---

### 2. Delivers a quick win or immediate value
**What a 5 looks like:** Achievable in ≤ 30 minutes (or Day 1 of a challenge). The user feels progress fast.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Takes days or weeks to apply and see a result.

---

### 3. Has high perceived value
**What a 5 looks like:** People would pay for this. It promises a tangible, visible outcome.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Feels cheap, like filler content, or is difficult to understand what you get.

---

### 4. Easy to consume
**What a 5 looks like:** Easy to read and scan. Appropriate for mobile. Clear visual hierarchy.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Long, dense, no variety, hard to skim.

---

### 5. Well positioned in the funnel (Funnel Psychology)
**What a 5 looks like:** Naturally leads the user toward the next step in the funnel and toward a sale.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Solves a problem that is unrelated to the core offer — no bridge to conversion.

---

### 6. Landing page uses psychological triggers
**What a 5 looks like:** Landing page includes results, curiosity, FOMO, and social proof. The user feels something.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Landing page is informational only — it describes the content but creates no emotional pull.

---

### 7. Has a compelling hook or unique mechanism
**What a 5 looks like:** Uses a unique mechanism or fresh angle that stands out in the niche. Feels proprietary.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Content that can be found easily elsewhere — rewritten or copied ideas with no distinctive angle.

---

### 8. Professional, clean design
**What a 5 looks like:** Strong branding, clean layout, no errors or inconsistencies.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Poor formatting, weak or inconsistent branding.

---

### 9. Strong headline and a single, clear call to action
**What a 5 looks like:** Benefit-focused headline (≤ 12 words) + one strong, clear CTA.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Vague headline; multiple CTAs competing for attention; unclear what to do next.

---

### 10. We have a plan to track results and key success indicators
**What a 5 looks like:** A plan or system is in place to measure performance — downloads, conversion rate, lead quality, funnel progression, and downstream revenue impact.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** No tracking plan, no KPIs defined.

|---|---|

| **31–40** | 🟡 Good but refine — tighten copy and/or design |

| **0–20** | ⛔ Start over — alignment or promise is fundamentally off |

## Suggested additions (not in original scorecard)

The following criteria are recommended additions based on the Point Numera tool philosophy and commercial context:

### 11. No email wall before value (PN tool rule)
**What a 5 looks like:** The user gets a real, useful output before any capture prompt appears. Email or CTA comes after value is delivered.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Email is required before the user can access anything meaningful.

### 12. Result interprets, not just calculates
**What a 5 looks like:** The tool or asset explains what the result means, what would change it, and what to do next — not just a number or a data dump.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Output is a raw number or list with no context or guidance.

### 13. Fits PN brand and tone
**What a 5 looks like:** Dark theme, correct colour tokens, PT Sans/Ancizar Sans, plain-language labels, founder-friendly tone. Looks like it belongs on pointnumera.com.
**What a 0–1 looks like:** Generic SaaS aesthetic, white backgrounds, blue/purple palette, finance jargon.
